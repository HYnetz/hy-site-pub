The event will cover the procurement process and indicative timelines, provide an overview of the assignment’s scope, and outline the key qualification and evaluation criteria, with particular attention to areas that may require clarification or are commonly misunderstood. It will also highlight common mistakes observed in past bidding processes to help participants better prepare their submissions. For more information, please consult this document.
