On 16 May, expert speakers from the Council of Europe and the University of Oslo showed us how digital technologies, including AI and open data, are transforming judicial systems. Almost 350 participants had a chance to discover how these innovations enhance transparency, accessibility, and the management of legal knowledge. The session highlighted the role of open judicial data in fostering a fairer legal system, the impact of AI on legal decision-making, and the importance of adhering to human rights standards.
