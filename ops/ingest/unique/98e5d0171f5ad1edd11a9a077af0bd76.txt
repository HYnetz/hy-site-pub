The Design and Build of Water Networks in Northern Al-Salt Areas / Balqa Governorate aims to upgrade and expand water networks to improve service delivery and reduce non-revenue water. The Design and Build of Water Networks in Al Hizam DZ02 focuses on the development of water infrastructure, including the design and construction of new water distribution networks.
