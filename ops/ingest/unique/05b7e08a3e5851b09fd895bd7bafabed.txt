However, the authorities encouraged the sustainability of the fishing activity by giving priority to the supply of the domestic market. The drop in activity has affected the income of fishermen less seriously than expected. In contrast, the shock of COVID-19 has severely affected workers in fish processing industries, especially the canned fish production. This note presents the main findings from a rapid diagnosis of the impact of COVID-19 on the fisheries sector and its vulnerability.
