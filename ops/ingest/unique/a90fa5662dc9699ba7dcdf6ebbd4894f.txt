Respondents include all operating and idle petroleum refineries (including new refineries under construction), non-refinery operators of distillation, reforming, cracking, coking, and hydrotreating processes, and refineries shut down during the previous year. These refineries are located in the 50 States, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, and other U.S. possessions.
