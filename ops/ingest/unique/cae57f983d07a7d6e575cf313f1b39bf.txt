Hideki Matsunaga Home Who We Are Experts Hideki Matsunaga Hideki Matsunaga Country Manager for Somalia Contact Information Delta Center, Menengai Road, Upper Hill, PO Box 30577-00100, Nairobi, Kenya vamani@worldbank.org Related Information The World Bank in Federal Republic of Somalia Mr. Hideki Matsunaga, a respected Japanese national, has been appointed as the World Bank Country Manager for Somalia, stationed in Nairobi. With a career that spans several decades, Mr. Matsunaga brings extensive experience and expertise to this crucial role, highlighting his exceptional leadership and strategic insight. Mr. Matsunaga's professional journey is marked by significant contributions in various fields, including operations, strategy, policy, research, and program management. His tenure at the World Bank has been distinguished by impactful roles, notably as an Advisor in the Chief Economist’s Office for the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region. In this capacity, he provided invaluable insights and guidance, contributing to the region's economic development and strategic planning. Before his current appointment, Mr. Matsunaga served as the Chief Representative of the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) in Kyiv, Ukraine. His leadership in this role was crucial in fostering international cooperation and advancing development initiatives, demonstrating his ability to navigate complex geopolitical landscapes and drive meaningful change. Mr. Matsunaga's career includes senior roles at several prestigious organizations, such as JICA, the Japan Bank for International Cooperation, Japan Platform, and the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP). His assignments have taken him to diverse global locations, including Tokyo, Cairo, and Amman, where he has consistently demonstrated his capacity to lead complex projects and initiatives with a focus on sustainable development and international collaboration. As Mr. Matsunaga takes on his new role, he is set to continue strengthening partnerships with the government and development partners in Somalia. His appointment marks a new chapter in the World Bank's commitment to supporting Somalia's development goals and enhancing the impact of its programs in the region. With his extensive experience and dedication, Mr. Matsunaga is well-equipped to uphold the high standards of the World Bank and advance the organization's mission in Somalia.
