In this role, he is responsible for fostering client relationships and coordinating and delivering their technical advisory engagements under the Reserve Advisory and Management Partnership (RAMP). Prior to joining the World Bank in 2022, he worked at the Central Bank of Albania for 15 years, where he managed reserves and led the Risk and Performance Analytics unit, followed by the Strategic Asset Allocation and Credit Risk unit within the Monetary Operations department. During this time, he was an adjunct professor of Portfolio Management at the University of Tirana and taught Principles of Investing at the University of New York Tirana, contributing to various books on these topics.
