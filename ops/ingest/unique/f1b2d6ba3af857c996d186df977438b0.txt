Event | Cameroon: Pre-Bid Briefing for Suppliers Specializing in Railway construction, supply, inspection, and logistics Apple Calendar Google Calendar Outlook Calendar Yahoo Calendar Outlook.com Calendar Office 365 Calendar Home News Events Cameroon: Pre-Bid Briefing for Suppliers Specializing in Railway construction, supply, inspection, and logistics Share Overview The Government of Cameroon, with financing from international partners including the World Bank, EIB, AFD, and the EU, is organizing a pre-bid supplier briefing for projects to rehabilitate and modernize railway infrastructure along the Douala–N’Djamena corridor and the Bélabo–Ngaoundéré section. Led by CAMRAIL, this event invites companies specializing in railway construction, supply, signaling, inspection, and logistics to express their interest, provide feedback on technical and operational challenges, and help shape the upcoming procurement process. Participation is for information-sharing only and does not confer any competitive advantage in future tenders. For more information, see here .
