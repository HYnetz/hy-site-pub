The third edition of the World Reconstruction Conference (WRC3) served as a call to action in order to promote resilience through post-crisis recovery. Over 800 experts and practitioners joined together June 6-8, 2017, in Brussels, to share best practices and lessons on recovery and explore how it can reduce future risk and promote sustainable poverty reduction.
