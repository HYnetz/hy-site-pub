Her research covers topics in infrastructure, labor, urban, and health economics, with an emphasis on how infrastructure investments shape worker outcomes. As part of the infrastructure unit of Development Impact, she is evaluating the impacts of electric mobility, energy access and transition, and digital skills on workers’ employment, health, and welfare.
