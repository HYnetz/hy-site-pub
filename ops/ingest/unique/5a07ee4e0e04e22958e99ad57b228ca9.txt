The baseline was conducted in March-August 2019 and a high-frequency phone survey series was launched on the sample in April 2020 with three rounds completed as of June 2021. The series of briefs from the follow-up’s present findings on key labor market, food security and education outcomes in the context of COVID-19. Briefs 1-4 produced from the original survey have been included to provide pre-COVID context. 1. Study Overview Baseline: Mar-Aug 2019.
