He is currently based in Dakar, Senegal. He has worked extensively on trade policy issues in Africa, first with WBI and then as trade coordinator for West Africa. He has been responsible for the preparation of seven Diagnostic Trade Integration Studies in West Africa, and is very familiar with the Enhanced Integrated Framework. Recently he completed a study on trade and growth in Côte d'Ivoire. He is now the lead economist for Senegal, Guinea Bissau, Mauritania, Cape Verde and The Gambia.
