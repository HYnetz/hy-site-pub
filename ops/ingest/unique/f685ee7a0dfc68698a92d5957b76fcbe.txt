The Success of Community-Based Childcare in Cambodia and Elderly Supporty in Viet Nam Who We Are News This page in: English 日本語 This page in: English 日本語 Video March 25, 2024 Supporting Young Children and Elderly in Cambodia and Viet Nam Share more close linkedin digg renren stumbleupon delicious sina A project supported by the Japan Social Development Fund (JSDF) recently helped establish nine community-based childcare centers in Cambodia for the children of garment factory workers. The project resulted in improved employment and labor outcomes, reduced unpaid household childcare an improved child development. In Viet Nam, a JSDF-supported project helped increase the participation of the elderly in income-generating activities and community-based health and social care services.
