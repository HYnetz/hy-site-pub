The project aims to consult market stakeholders to gather their opinions on the work involved in these upcoming contracts. The objectives of this Notice of Early Market Engagement are as follows: • To assess market stakeholders' interest in the proposed project; • Gather feedback on key project requirements, risks, and opportunities; • Identify potential industry partners.
