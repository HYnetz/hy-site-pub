The Malagasy State, concerned about protecting the population from higher prices, sets with the oil companies a maximum price of fuel at the pump which is sometimes below the real cost of supply. The State pays the difference between the price at the pump and the real supply cost. However, by spending a great deal of money to limit the increase in fuel prices, the Government is reducing resources available to support pro-poor policies.
