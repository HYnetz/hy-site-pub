Eugenia Rodríguez Home Who We Are Experts Eugenia Rodríguez Eugenia Rodríguez Economist, Enterprise Analysis Unit, Global Indicators Group Eugenia Rodríguez joined the Enterprise Analysis Unit in 2023. She holds a Master of Public Policy with a concentration in International Development from the Sanford School of Public Policy of Duke University. She also graduated from an advanced education program in Quantitative Methods and holds a BSc in Economics from Universidad ORT Uruguay. Prior to joining the unit, she worked in economics research, policy, and diplomacy. Her interests are at the intersection of international development, trade, public policy, and economics.
