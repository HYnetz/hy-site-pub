Transforming logistics in India: Multimodal freight corridor to revolutionize Gangetic plain Who We Are News Video December 5, 2024 Transforming logistics in India: Multimodal freight corridor to revolutionize Gangetic plain Share more close linkedin digg renren stumbleupon delicious sina India's logistics infrastructure is being transformed through a multimodal freight corridor along the Gangetic Plain. This revolutionary project integrates road, rail, and waterways, reducing transport costs, boosting efficiency, and cutting carbon emissions. From the Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor (EDFC) to the revived Ganga waterway, these advancements are enabling faster goods movement, creating jobs, and fostering economic growth, making India a competitive global business hub.
