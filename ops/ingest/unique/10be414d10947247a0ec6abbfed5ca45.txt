These notes complete the recently released Western Balkans Regular Economic Report (RER) #17 and accompanying Country Notes - launched on April 29 and May 29 - and show how the macroeconomic impact of COVID-19 is projected to impact the Western Balkans. This final group of notes highlight specific sector topics – fiscal, financial, and external sector policies and the private sector impact as reported by firms - that are critical for an adequate short and medium-term policy response to the impacts of COVID-19. The latest notes are: The Economic and Social Impact of COVID-19 on Fiscal Policy The Economic and Social Impact of COVID-19 on the Financial Sector The Economic and Social Impact of COVID-19 on the External Sector Covid-19 Vulnerability in the Western Balkans: A Firm-Level Perspective The complete Western Balkans RER #17 now provides a full picture of how the COVID-19 pandemic has impacted the Western Balkans and advises on how to design adequate macro, social, and sector-specific policy response options for the region in the short- and medium-term.
