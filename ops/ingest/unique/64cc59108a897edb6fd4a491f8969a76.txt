Event | 32nd PPA Annual Conference and Trade Exhibition Apple Calendar Google Calendar Outlook Calendar Yahoo Calendar Outlook.com Calendar Office 365 Calendar Home News Events 32nd PPA Annual Conference and Trade Exhibition Share Overview The PPA conference is the largest energy conference in the region, focusing on all aspects of electricity in the Pacific Islands. The conference will offer participants a combination of speeches, donor presentations, technical presentations, and more. This year’s conference will be held at the Ngarachamayong Cultural Centre in Koror, Palau from September 22 – September 25, 2025. The PPA Conference will offer delegates a combination of speeches, donor presentations, technical presentations, Utility Board Members workshop and the Trade Exhibition of products and services by our Allied Members. Attendees will include representatives of Department of Resources development, the project manager for ARISE, the CEO of Marshall Islands energy company. The early market engagement presentation will be held as a hybrid portion of the event (virtual and in-person). For more information, see here .
