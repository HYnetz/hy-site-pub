David Wilson Home Who We Are Experts David Wilson David Wilson Global Director, Health, Nutrition & Population, Health, Nutrition and Population, HNP AREAS OF EXPERTISE Health David Wilson is currently the Global Director, Health, Nutrition & Population Global Practice. He served as Team Leader of the World Bank’s COVID-19 and Health Emergency Preparedness and Response and as the World Bank’s Global AIDS Director. He has published 150 odd scientific papers and worked in about 60 countries on all continents, including 28 countries in Africa and many fragile and conflict settings in Africa, the Middle East, South Asia, East Asia and the Pacific.
