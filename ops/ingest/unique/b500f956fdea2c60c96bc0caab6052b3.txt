The project represents one of the largest public logistics infrastructure investments in North Macedonia, financed by the World Bank. The objective of this session is to present the upcoming procurement process, outline the main qualification and evaluation requirements, and provide early insight into how interested companies can participate effectively in the forthcoming tender. By attending, participants will gain a clear understanding of the project’s scope, expected contractor capabilities, tendering procedures, and compliance obligations under World Bank procurement standards. This webinar is specifically designed for construction companies, contractors, engineering firms, joint ventures, and consortia with experience in large-scale infrastructure projects, logistics facilities, industrial parks, or agri-food sector developments. It will also be of value to consultants, suppliers, and potential partners seeking to engage in future stages of the project. The event aims to foster transparency, inclusiveness, and meaningful engagement with the international construction market while ensuring that the procurement process is aligned with best practices and market realities. Participants will have the opportunity to learn about the technical scope of works, qualification criteria, submission requirements, and overall procurement structure. The webinar will take place virtually on November 27, 2025, at 14:00 CET via Microsoft Teams. The meeting link and agenda are attached to this invitation .
