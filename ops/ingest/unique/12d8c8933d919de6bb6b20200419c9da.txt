Join the upcoming three-part data.europa academy data visualisation training series ‘Visualising data for impact' , taking place from 14 to 16 October 2025 , each afternoon from 16:30 to 18:00 CEST . The training will be led by visualisation designer and professor Alberto Cairo . With years of experience and several books on infographics and data visualisation, Alberto will guide you through the three episodes, each focusing on a different element of visualisation: Episode 2: ‘ Designing with integrity ’, scheduled for 15 October, will discover how to spot and avoid common pitfalls that can distort data. Episode 3: ‘ Data storytelling and creating your narrative ’, on 16 October, focuses on visual design and the narrative around it to unlock the full creative potential of data storytelling. By participating in this training, you will gain skills to create accurate, ethical and impactful data visualisations and feel empowered to start building meaning from your own data. To get the full value of this series, we highly recommend to register for all three episodes . Each episode builds on the last, helping you master the principles of data visualisation and grow into a visual storyteller.
