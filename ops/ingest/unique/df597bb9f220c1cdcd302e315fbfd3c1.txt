I. Package A – Design, Reengineering, Development, and Deployment of E-Services and Integration with the Government Digital Ecosystem. II. Package B – Engagement of a System Integrator for the Deployment, Customization, and Integration of Foundational Digital ID and CRVS Core Modules, including Data Migration, Supply, and Configuration of Server, Backend Infrastructure, and Enrollment Kits. This engagement is intended to provide early visibility into the planned scope of work and evaluation approach, and to gather feedback from industry stakeholders to help refine the procurement strategy, associated risks and technical requirements.
