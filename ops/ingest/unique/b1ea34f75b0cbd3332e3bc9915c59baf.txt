Giving Cambodia’s Children a Smart, Healthy Start Who We Are News Video September 12, 2019 Giving Cambodia’s Children a Smart, Healthy Start Share more close linkedin digg renren stumbleupon delicious sina One in three Cambodian children under age five are stunted. Childhood malnutrition and micronutrient deficiencies, some of which are passed on by a child’s own undernourished mother, are also a concern. To reduce stunting, all children must receive nutritious food, quality, affordable health care, access to safe drinking water and sanitation, adequate family income, and nurturing care.
