Labor Mobility, Migration, and COVID-19 (Part 2) Who We Are News Video May 14, 2020 Labor Mobility, Migration, and COVID-19 (Part 2) Share more close linkedin digg renren stumbleupon delicious sina Border closures, social distancing measures, and economic lockdowns are among the standard and immediate policies being implemented in the fight against COVID-19. The impact on labor markets—especially on migrant workers and their employers—has been swift and severe. In this e-seminar on May 14, 2020, Mushfiq Mobarak (Professor of Economics, Yale University), discussed preliminary results of several projects in Bangladesh, Nepal, Nigeria, and Sierra Leone to develop evidence-based policy responses to COVID-19 using migration and mobility data. Then Erhan Artuc (Senior Economist, World Bank) presented a dynamic labor mobility framework that incorporates an epidemiological model to explore the effectiveness of various suppression mechanisms such as border and business closures.
