Published in "World Development" by Jonathan Fox, Rachel Robinson, and Naomi Hossain, the study uses the state-society synergy approach to analyze exceptional cases when reformers within the state managed to deliver openings for citizen action that tangibly empowered otherwise excluded or marginalized groups.
