Reconstructed by the Kabul Municipality under the Kabul Urban Transport Efficiency Improvement (KUTEI) Project, this road connects Parwan-e-II square and Sarak-e-5 Square. As one of the main roads of Kabul city, it has eased access of nearby municipality districts to other parts of the capital city. With funding support from the Afghanistan Reconstruction Trust Fund (ARTF), KUTEI targets improvement of transport services through a package of interrelated interventions to build Kabul Municipality’s capacity.
