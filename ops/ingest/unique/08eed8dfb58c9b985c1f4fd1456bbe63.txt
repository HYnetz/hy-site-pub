At the root of this vulnerability is the country’s limited adaptive capacity. Despite the country’s strained fiscal and institutional context, the cost of inaction is high. Critical no-regret investments in key service sectors like energy, water, transport and solid waste are urgently needed in the short-term to mitigate the impact of climate change on Lebanon’s development path.
