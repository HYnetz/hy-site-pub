This is the second tranche of the $20 million approved in February 2019 to support the implementation of the joint Strategic Response Plan 3.0 , covering the period from February to July 2019. In line with the PEF’s quick disbursement arrangements, the funds were disbursed within three days of the Steering Body’s approval.
