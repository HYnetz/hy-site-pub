Join us to learn more about the factors that drive success when building local AI and digital solutions to scale and transform service delivery. Together, we will uncover best practices from Irembo’s rollout of AI-enhanced government services in Rwanda, explore the real-world obstacles that African AI innovators encounter, and examine how initiatives like the World Bank’s Digital DPI and Services program are shaping the future of digital innovation on the continent.
