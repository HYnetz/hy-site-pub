Purchasing power parities (PPPs) provide a clearer picture of global development, poverty, and inequality. The International Comparison Program, or ICP, is one of the world’s largest statistical collaborations—bringing together a multitude of agencies and experts to measure and compare economic activity across nearly 200 economies. Learn how the ICP is shaping the future of global economic measurement—ensuring that better data leads to better policies, smarter investments, and more informed decisions worldwide.
