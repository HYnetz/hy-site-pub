Jones Arkoh Paintsil Home Who We Are Experts Jones Arkoh Paintsil Jones Arkoh Paintsil Consultant, Enterprise Analysis Unit, Global Indicators Group Jones joined the Enterprise Analysis Unit as a consultant in December 2024. He is a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at Howard University and previously worked as a District Data Quality Monitor for Ghana’s 2021 Population and Housing Census. He holds a BA in Economics and Mathematics, and an MPhil in Economics from the University of Cape Coast, Ghana. His research focuses on applied microeconomics, particularly development and labor economics, with publications on financial liberalization, maternal health insurance, and intimate partner violence in Sub-Saharan Africa.
