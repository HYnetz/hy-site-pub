Menu Data Metrics Open Government Contact User Guide User Guide Back to updates Agencies get how-to on open data compliance | FCW August 20, 2013 Agencies are expected to deliver on several elements of President Barack Obama’s executive order on open data by Nov. 1, including creation and publication of a list of data assets. To steer these efforts, the Office of Management and Budget and the Office of Science and Technology Policy released guidance that gets into the weeds about how agencies can fulfill the directives and incorporate open data policy into everyday activities.
