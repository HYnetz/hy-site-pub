Menu Data Metrics Open Government Contact User Guide User Guide Back to climate , updates NASA Launches Earth Science Challenges with OpenNEX Cloud Data By Data.gov admins · June 24, 2014 NASA is launching two challenges to give the public an opportunity to create innovative ways to use data from the agency’s Earth science satellites. The challenges will use the Open NASA Earth Exchange. OpenNEX is a data, supercomputing and knowledge platform where users can share modeling and analysis codes, scientific results, knowledge and expertise to solve big data challenges in the Earth sciences. A component of the NASA Earth Exchange, OpenNEX provides users a large collection of climate and Earth science satellite data sets, including global land surface images, vegetation conditions, climate observations and climate projections. For more information, see the NASA OpenNEX Press Release and the OpenNEX site.
