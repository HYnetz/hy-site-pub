Event | Korea-World Bank Partnership Facility (KWPF) 2025 Global Forum: Innovation in Agriculture and Food Systems Apple Calendar Google Calendar Outlook Calendar Yahoo Calendar Outlook.com Calendar Office 365 Calendar Home News Events KWPF 2025 Global Forum: Innovation in Agriculture and Food Systems Share The Korea-World Bank Partnership Facility (KWPF) , in collaboration with the Global Department of Agriculture and Food at the World Bank, will host the 2025 Global Forum on Innovation in Agriculture and Food Systems on May 27, 2025, in Busan, Republic of Korea. This one-day event will feature some of the most impactful KWPF-funded projects, highlighting the KWPF’s role as a catalyst for innovation in agriculture and food systems. The forum will bring together key stakeholders, thought leaders, and practitioners from around the world to explore advancements in agribusiness and entrepreneurship, climate-smart agriculture, agricultural infrastructure, digital agriculture and technology, and efforts to improve food quality and reduce loss and waste—all with the aim of driving more sustainable, resilient, and inclusive food systems. KWPF supports countries that seek to adapt and apply Korea’s development experience and technical expertise to achieve their own development goals. Over the past decade, KWPF has received US$468 million in contributions and supported 208 grant projects across various sectors. Contacts Jacqueline Purtell Kistler Washington DC 1800 G Street, N.W.
