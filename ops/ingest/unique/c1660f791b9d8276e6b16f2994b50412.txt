Tasmia Rahman Home Who We Are Experts Tasmia Rahman Tasmia Rahman Behavioral Scientist RELATED World Bank Mind, Behavior, and Development (eMBeD) Unit Development Impact (DIME) AREAS OF EXPERTISE Evidence-Based Public Policy Gender Labor Markets Social Protection and Labor Poverty Tasmia Rahman is a Behavioral Scientist at the World Bank’s Mind, Behavior & Development Unit (eMBeD), within Development Impact (DIME). Her work includes designing and evaluating behavioral interventions across various policy areas, such as labor and entrepreneurship, social protection, financial inclusion, social norms, and health and nutrition. Prior to joining the Bank, she worked on impact evaluation and implementation of labor market, tax compliance, and digital finance projects in East and West Africa and South Asia with the Georgetown University Initiative on Innovation, Development and Evaluation (Gui2de) and BRAC.
