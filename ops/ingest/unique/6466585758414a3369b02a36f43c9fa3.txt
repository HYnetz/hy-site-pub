Only half of Cameroon’s population has access to electricity, and those who have access to power often experience lengthy outages. The upcoming 420 MW Nachtigal Hydropower Plant will help Cameroon increase its electricity generating capacity by 30% and will replace expensive thermal energy with cheaper renewable hydropower energy.
