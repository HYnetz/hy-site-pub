The two-day event will continue sensitization and awareness efforts to clarify procurement processes and broaden access to public procurement opportunities across the region. The objectives are to: Provide clear guidance to firms on how to bid for opportunities for WB and CDB financed projects; Enable executing agencies to present upcoming procurement opportunities to the private sector; Deliver targeted sessions, including, a detailed walkthrough of procurement processes by WB and CDB, and engage boundary partners to ensure participation from women owned/controlled businesses and other underrepresented groups in public procurement.
