Her research lies at the intersection of development and urban economics, with a focus on how mobility and public transportation systems in developing countries impact economic outcomes. Her work uses administrative data, high-frequency mobility data, and randomized control trials to study how barriers to mobility affect welfare, market integration, and access to jobs.
