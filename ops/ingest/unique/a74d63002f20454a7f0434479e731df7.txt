Event | 5th Anniversary WDR 2020: Trading for Development in the Age of Global Value Chains Apple Calendar Google Calendar Outlook Calendar Yahoo Calendar Outlook.com Calendar Office 365 Calendar Home News Events 5th Anniversary Event | WDR 2020: Trading for Development in the Age of Global Value Chains Share Overview Agenda Related The 2020 World Development Report explored how global value chains (GVCs) could drive development. Five years later, trade is being reshaped by geopolitical tensions, climate pressures, and technological shifts. This event revisits the WDR’s insights and asks how GVC-based development can evolve in a more fragmented global economy. Organizers Penny Goldberg (Yale University), Erhan Artuc (Development Research Group), Daria Taglioni (Development Research Group). Questions about the event content may be addressed to wdr2020_anniversaryconference@worldbank.org .
