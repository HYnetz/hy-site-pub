It highlights the transformative role of integrity compliance in driving sustainable development. At a time when ethical business practices are critical for sustainable job creation, this event showcases how integrity compliance has evolved from a regulatory obligation into a catalyst of private sector growth, increased market access, and job opportunities. It also commemorates 15 years of the establishment of the World Bank Group Integrity Compliance Office, the adoption of incorporating integrity conditions into sanctions, and the Multilateral Development Bank’s cross-debarment agreement – all of which have played a pivotal role in shaping global standards for ethical business conduct. Through keynote remarks, an engaging fireside chat, and insightful expert panels, participants will explore: The evolution and impact of the World Bank Group Sanctions System over the past 15 years How integrity compliance helps drive private sector growth, job creation, and greater access to capital and markets Practical examples of companies leveraging integrity compliance to scale operations and create jobs The launch of the revised World Bank Group Integrity Compliance Guidelines and how the Integrity Compliance Knowledge Sharing Platform supports global implementation Join us as we celebrate the achievements of the past, reaffirm our shared commitment to transparency and accountability, and highlight how anticorruption frameworks have helped unlock investment, build trust, and foster inclusive economic opportunities. 8:30 – 9:00 a.m. (ET) Registration 9:00 – 9:10 a.m. Welcome and Opening Remarks 9:10 – 10:10 a.m. Panel Discussion The Evolving Role of the World Bank’s Two-Tier Sanctions System: A Catalyst for Sustainable Growth and Ethical Business 10:10 – 10:50 a.m. Fireside Chat Driving Change Through Integrity Compliance: A Conversation on Impact, Lessons, and the Future 10:50 – 10:55 a.m. Video Presentation from the World Bank Group Integrity Compliance Office 10:55 – 11:10 a.m. Coffee Break 11:10 a.m. – 12:10 p.m. Panel Discussion Why Integrity Programs Matter for Accessing Capital, Markets, and Job Creation 12:10 – 12:25 p.m. Introduction and Launch of revised World Bank Group Integrity Compliance Guidelines 12:25 – 12:35 p.m. Introduction of Integrity Compliance Knowledge Sharing Platform 12:35 – 12:45 p.m. Closing Coming Soon FAQs Do I need to register to attend the event?
