Pswarayi-Riddihough is wrapping up her assignment as the World Bank Country Director for Maldives, Nepal and Sri Lanka. As she moves to Maputo from Colombo to take up her new role as the Country Director for Mozambique, Madagascar, Mauritius, Seychelles, and Comoros, we look back at the main takeaways from her four years of working with the Maldivian authorities to help achieve shared prosperity for the Maldivian people.
