Tommy Chrimes Home Who We Are Experts Tommy Chrimes Tommy Chrimes Senior Economist, Development Economics, DEC CONTACT tchrimes@worldbank.org RELATED Prospects Group website AREAS OF EXPERTISE Global Economy Aid Effectiveness Demographic Change Sustainable Development Tommy Chrimes is a Senior Economist in the World Bank’s Prospects Group, where he contributes to a range of surveillance and analytical products on global economic and development issues. Previously, he served as an Advisor to the UK Executive Directors at the International Monetary Fund and World Bank Group Executive Boards. Earlier in his career, he worked within the UK civil service. In 2024, Tommy coauthored The Great Reversal: Prospects, Risks, and Policies in International Development Association Countries , published by the World Bank.
