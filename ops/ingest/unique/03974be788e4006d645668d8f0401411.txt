Pagma Genden Home Who We Are Experts Pagma Genden Pagma Genden Operations Officer Pagma Genden is an Operations Officer in the Health, Nutrition, and Population Global Practice at the World Bank. She coordinates the World Bank Group (WBG) activities in Mongolia's health and education sectors. Pagma is an economist with broad project management experience. Before joining the World Bank Group in 2013 as an Extended Term Consultant, she worked on poverty alleviation and sustainable livelihood projects funded by the WBG and Swiss Development Cooperation Agency in Mongolia. She also worked for the Government of Mongolia and participated in the policy and legislation development and implementation of national programs on poverty and unemployment reduction and social protection.
