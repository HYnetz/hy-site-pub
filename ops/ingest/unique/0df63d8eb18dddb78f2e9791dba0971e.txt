The current World Bank portfolio in Paraguay focuses on four main areas: supporting human capital development and sustainable growth, improving market access for agricultural producer organizations and indigenous communities, enhancing the quality and safety of selected routes to improve connectivity and efficiency, and strengthening primary health care to provide better, more accessible health services for all Paraguayans. Together, we are working to build a stronger and more resilient Paraguay for both the present and future generations.
