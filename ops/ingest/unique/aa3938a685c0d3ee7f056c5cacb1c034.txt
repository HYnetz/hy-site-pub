On the 13 of June, experts from the Polish Academy of Sciences and the Marine Analyst took a deep dive into how open marine data can empower researchers, policymakers, and industry professionals to collaborate more effectively, drive innovation, and make evidence-based decisions. Our first speaker, Pascal Derycke , provided insights into the case study of Marine Analyst, a collaborative platform that simplifies access to global marine datasets and analytical tools. Aleksandra Dudkowska's presentation centered on creating a digital twin of the Southern Baltic Sea, a groundbreaking project dedicated to enhancing management of the Polish coastline.
