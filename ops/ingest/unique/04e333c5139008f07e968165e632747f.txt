Georgia Joins IDA to Fight Poverty Who We Are News Video September 16, 2022 Georgia Joins IDA to Fight Poverty Share more close linkedin digg renren stumbleupon delicious sina On September 13, Georgia officially joined the International Development Association (IDA) as a donor at a signing ceremony that was part of a conference celebrating 30 years of partnership between the country and the World Bank. Georgia originally joined IDA as a beneficiary in 1993, receiving $1.53 billion in financial support before graduating from the association in 2014. Georgia’s new donor status at IDA represents a major milestone in the country’s economic transformation, marking the first time it has become a donor to any international financial institution.
