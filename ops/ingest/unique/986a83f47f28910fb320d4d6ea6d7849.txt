MDBs are also committing to working with partner countries to implement national-level reforms in order to mainstream sustainability into domestic procurement activity. Governments spend US$13 trillion annually -around 15% of global GDP- which highlights the important role public procurement will play in achieving global sustainability goals. MDBs will work with partner countries to achieve a broad set of sustainability objectives, including achieving the goals set out in the Paris Agreement with a particular focus on decarbonizing supply chains, closing gender gaps and increasing inclusion.
