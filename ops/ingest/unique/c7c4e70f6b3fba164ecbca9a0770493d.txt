Rupes manages the Panel’s website and social media which aims to build better awareness of the Panel as an independent accountability mechanism among its key stakeholders particularly the project affected communities and Civil Society Organizations. Prior to joining the Panel, Rupes has worked in the World Bank Executive Director's Office for Bangladesh, Bhutan, India and Sri Lanka, Asian Development Bank, HCL Technologies and Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance, Government of India.
