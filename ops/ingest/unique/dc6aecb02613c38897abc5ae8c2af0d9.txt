It is a central component of gender equality, alongside increasing human and physical endowments and expanding economic opportunities. Voice is having a say in the processes that affect one’s life and environment. Agency is the ability to make choices to achieve desired outcomes. Expanding women’s voice and agency in many life domains is critical to ending extreme poverty and boosting shared prosperity. Learn more about the World Bank’s new report on Women’s Voice, Agency and Participation and see examples of work in promoting collective action through Community-Driven Development .
