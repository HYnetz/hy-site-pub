RTOs are independent, membership-based, non-profit organizations that ensure reliability and optimize supply and demand bids for wholesale electric power. Find more information on the U.S. RTOs and their data in the links provided below. Wholesale Electricity Market Portal Demo More about the Wholesale Electricity Markets on EIA.gov The Wholesale Electricity Market Portal was launched by the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) in March of 2024 to help users examine and access electricity markets data in the seven Regional Transmission Organizations (RTO) and Independent System Operators (ISO). Independent System Operator - New England (ISO-NE) New York Independent System Operator (NYISO) PJM Interconnection (PJM) Midcontinent Independent System Operator (MISO) Southwest Power Pool (SPP) Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT) California Independent System Operator (CAISO) Key data sets include: Day-ahead locational marginal prices (LMP) Real-time LMPs Actual load/demand Forecast load/demand Generation fuel mix Interchange Temperature of major cities Wholesale Electricity and Natural Gas Market Data Data on weighted-average prices for electricity and natural gas traded at hubs and delivery points in North America and prices republished with permission, from data collected by the Intercontinental Exchange (ICE), updated biweekly. Hourly Electric Grid Monitor Up-to-the-hour information showing electricity demand and generation by source for 64 balancing authorities across the U.S. electric grid and hourly CO 2 emission estimates. Today in Energy : Regional Transmission Organizations Short, timely articles on energy facts, issues, and trends. Electricity Monthly Update : Regional Wholesale Markets Analysis and highlights from the Electric Power Monthly data. Data Availability The Portal data is extracted from data published by the RTO/ISOs. Data availability varies by data set and RTO/ISO. Some data is made available by the RTO/ISOs on a sub-hourly real-time basis. The most frequently the Portal will update data is hourly. Some data may be updated daily, weekly, monthly or in longer time periods. Data are available via the dashboards linked above as well as via flat files for bulk download. Data are not currently available via the API. Data Quality The data is gathered from RTO/ISOs and should be considered preliminary. EIA does not verify the quality of the data and it is made available "as-is". Neither EIA nor the RTO/ISOs are responsible for reliance on the data for any specific use. Data Use and Republication EIA accesses data from the RTO/ISOs publicly available websites and provides a limited history for public use.
