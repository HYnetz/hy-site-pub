Share more close linkedin digg renren stumbleupon delicious sina The Marshall Islands are a group of islands in the Pacific Ocean, home to over 50,000 people living across more than 1,000 islands. We hit the streets of the capital, Majuro, to find out what’s really possible for young Marshallese. This is part of a new World Bank report series, ‘Pacific Possible’, focusing on the challenges and opportunities for the region in coming decades.
