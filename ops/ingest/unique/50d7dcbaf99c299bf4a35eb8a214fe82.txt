He also provides advice on strategic asset allocation and risk management. Keyvan joined the World Bank RAMP from the Treasury middle office, where he spent seven years working on strategic asset allocation, risk management, structured finance, derivatives, and financial modeling. Keyvan brings over 15 years of professional experience in financial markets. Before joining the World Bank, he worked at Fannie Mae and T. Rowe Price in various roles focusing on mortgages, debt, equity, and derivative markets. Keyvan holds a master’s degree in applied mathematics and finance from the University of California-Davis.
