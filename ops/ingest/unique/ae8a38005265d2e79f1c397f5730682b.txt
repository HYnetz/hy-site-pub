Franka Braun Home Who We Are Experts Franka Braun Franka Braun Senior Natural Resource Management Specialist Contact Franka Braun Maputo, Mozambique fbraun@worldbank.org RELATED Mozambique's Integrated Landscape Management Portfolio (ILM) Franka Braun, a German national, is a Senior Natural Resource Management Specialist who is working on land use, forestry and blue economy programs in Mozambique. Prior to her posting in Africa, she was based in Bogotá, focusing on initiatives promoting sustainable land use and reducing deforestation in Colombia and Mexico. Previous to the World Bank, she worked at Natsource Asset Management, where she developed and managed carbon finance projects around the world, and served as the company's specialist for forestry transactions. She has also worked at the UNDP on access to energy in Senegal. Franka holds a master's degree in Political Science from the Free University of Berlin and the Institut d’Etude Politique (IEP), and a master’s degree in Economic Policy and International Energy Policy Management from Columbia University.
