Monali Ranade Home Who We Are Experts Monali Ranade Monali Ranade Lead Energy Specialist for Eritrea, Ethiopia, South Sudan, and Sudan, Africa, AFR AREAS OF EXPERTISE Infrastructure Energy Monali Ranade is Lead Energy Specialist for Eritrea, Ethiopia, South Sudan, and Sudan and supports the Regional Energy Access program in Eastern and Southern Africa (ASCENT) under the Mission 300 initiative. Ms. Ranade has over seventeen years of experience at the World Bank. She was previously the World Bank’s Infrastructure Program Leader for Eritrea, Ethiopia, South Sudan, and Sudan, supporting strategic dialogue and operational engagements in Energy, Transport, Urban, Resilience and Land sectors. Prior to this position Ms. Ranade held other positions, including Senior Energy Specialist in the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region and as Senior Operations Officer in the Climate Change department. Before joining the Bank, Monali worked in Energy and Climate Consulting, with an interest in exploring innovative solutions for public and private sector institutions.
