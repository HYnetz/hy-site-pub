McLaughlin, a public health specialist who has worked on health projects mainly in Sub-Saharan Africa, South and East Asia and the South Pacific, conducted her Master’s and Doctoral studies in Public Health at Johns Hopkins University, and formerly worked as an epidemiologist for NGOs/PVOs on child health, disease control, and nutrition projects.
