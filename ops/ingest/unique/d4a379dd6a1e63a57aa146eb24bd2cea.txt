Her current work focuses on applying behavioral insights to improve health and environmental outcomes. Prior to joining the Bank, she worked as a senior research scientist at the University of Maryland’s Center for Health Information and Decision Systems leveraging behavioral science, technology, and analytics to solve healthcare challenges.
