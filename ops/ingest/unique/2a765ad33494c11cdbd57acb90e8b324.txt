Event | Request for Participation in Early Market Engagement for Procurement of: Istanbul North Rail Crossing Project (INRAIL) Apple Calendar Google Calendar Outlook Calendar Yahoo Calendar Outlook.com Calendar Office 365 Calendar Home News Events Request for Participation in Early Market Engagement for Procurement of: Istanbul North Rail Crossing Project (INRAIL) Share Overview The Government of the Republic of Türkiye intends to receive financing from the World Bank and other International Financial Institutions toward the cost of the Istanbul North Rail Crossing Project (INRAIL) and intends to apply part of the proceeds toward payments under the forthcoming contract(s) to be concluded through RFB process to be conducted after EME. The procurements will be conducted under the World Bank’s Procurement Regulations. The estimated project cost of INRAIL is US $8,100 million, inclusive of VAT, contingencies, construction supervision, and project management.
