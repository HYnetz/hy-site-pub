At the World Bank, Kristalina has led with judgment and determination, putting all her efforts into delivering our core mandate of reducing poverty and promoting shared and sustainable prosperity. She has rightly positioned the institution to promote growth and development, connecting our work to key agendas such as jobs, human capital, debt, trade, and technological disruption. Among her many accomplishments have been her leadership on supporting fragile countries; her commitment to women’s empowerment as a development issue; and her passion for addressing the effects of climate change.
