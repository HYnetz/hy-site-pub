Houman Najafi Home Who We Are Experts Houman Najafi Houman Najafi Senior Financial Officer, Capital Markets and Investments, Treasury, TRE RELATED Treasury Houman Najafi is a Senior Financial Officer for the World Bank Treasury based in Washington, DC. He is responsible for managing the relationships with bond investors, shareholders, and rating agencies for International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (IBRD) and the International Development Association (IDA), two of the World Bank Group institutions. He performs similar functions for the International Finance Facility for Immunisation (IFFIm), for which the World Bank acts as treasury manager.
