This new series brings together some of the most passionate voices in the Pacific community – thought leaders, activists, businesspeople and youth leaders – to tackle critical topics that will shape the future of the Pacific, with a focus on solutions. Co-hosted by the University of the South Pacific (USP), the World Bank and its sister organization, the International Finance Corporation , Future Pasifika is filmed in front of a live audience at the USP Laucala Bay Campus in Suva, Fiji, and livestreamed globally on Facebook and World Bank Live . Sign up to our Pacific Possible newsletter to hear about future episodes, and keep up with the conversation using the hashtag #FuturePasifika on Facebook and Twitter.
