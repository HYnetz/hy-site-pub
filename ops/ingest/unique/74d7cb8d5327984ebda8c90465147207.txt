New Dashboard to Track Food and Nutrition Security and Global Response Who We Are News Video November 7, 2022 Introducing the Global Food and Nutrition Security Dashboard Share more close linkedin digg renren stumbleupon delicious sina A global food crisis is unfolding, driven by conflicts, extreme climate events, high food prices. Swift action is critical. Germany and the World Bank, within the framework of the G7, launched the Global Alliance for Food Security, one key element of the alliance is the Global Food and Nutrition Security Dashboard. The Dashboard is designed to consolidate and present up-to-date data on food crisis severity, track global food security financing, and make available global and country-level research and analysis to improve coordination of the policy and financial response to the crisis.
