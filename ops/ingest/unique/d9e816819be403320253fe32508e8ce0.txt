International Women's Day 2025: World Bank Women Leading Change in Thailand Who We Are News FEATURE STORY March 27, 2025 International Women's Day 2025: World Bank Women Leading Change in Thailand Share more close linkedin digg renren stumbleupon delicious sina This year, the World Bank Thailand honors three remarkable women whose expertise and dedication are shaping Thailand’s future. Through a special Q&A feature on our website , we highlight their stories, challenges, and aspirations for advancing development in Thailand and beyond. Each of these women has taken on critical roles in addressing some of the most pressing challenges in Thailand and beyond— building sustainable cities, financing a sustainable future, and shaping economic policies. Their insights reflect the power of women in leadership and their ability to drive meaningful change. Shaping Thailand's Economic Future: Warunthorn Puthong, World Bank Economist for Thailand Thailand’s economy is at a pivotal moment, balancing growth, sustainability, and inclusivity in an ever-evolving global landscape. At the heart of these efforts is Warunthorn Puthong who works to provide data-driven insights, shape policy recommendations, and support strategies that drive long-term economic resilience. Read more about her journey as a woman in the field of economics and as she discusses Thailand’s economic outlook and the challenges and opportunities ahead. Building Sustainable Cities: Pichaya Moeller, World Bank Urban Development Consultant As Thailand’s cities grow and evolve, the challenge of creating livable, resilient, and inclusive urban spaces has never been more critical. At the forefront of this transformation is Pichaya Moeller who works to shape policies and projects that enhance urban sustainability and infrastructure. She shares her journey in urban development, the biggest challenges facing Thailand’s cities, and how innovative planning can create greener, smarter, and more inclusive urban spaces. Financing a Sustainable Future: Ornsaran Pomme Manuamorn, World Bank Senior Financial Sector Specialist As Thailand transitions toward a greener, more resilient economy, sustainable finance plays a crucial role in unlocking investments that drive environmental and social progress. Leading these efforts is Ornsaran Pomme Manuamorn who works to develop green finance, climate investment, and financial inclusion strategies that support Thailand’s sustainable development goals. She shares her insights on how sustainable finance is shaping Thailand’s economic future, the challenges and opportunities in mobilizing green investments, and her journey as a woman in the financial sector.
