QPSD: Quarterly Public Sector Debt Debt Statistics Quarterly Public Sector Debt (QPSD) Quarterly Public Sector Debt (QPSD) Home About Statistics Publications Methodology Debt Events Contact Us FAQ The Quarterly Public Sector Debt Statistics (QPSD) database, jointly developed by the World Bank and the International Monetary Fund, brings together detailed quarterly public sector debt data of selected countries. The main purpose of the database is to facilitate timely dissemination in standard formats of public sector debt data.
