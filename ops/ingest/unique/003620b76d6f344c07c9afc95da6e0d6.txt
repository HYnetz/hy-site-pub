He is a Ph.D. Candidate in Economics at McGill University in Montreal, Canada. He has worked for 2 years as a research assistant at ISID (Institute for the Study of International Development), helping to establish a link between policy analysts from developing countries and professors from McGill University to build research and analytical capacity. He holds an MA in Economics from the Université Laval, Canada, and a master’s degree in applied Statistics from ENSAE (National School of Statistics) of Dakar, Senegal.
