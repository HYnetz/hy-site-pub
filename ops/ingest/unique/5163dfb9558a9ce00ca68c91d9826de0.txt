She is one of the lead authors of the corporate flagship Global Economic Prospects report, which presents the World Bank’s view on the global economy. In 2024, she coauthored The Great Reversal: Prospects, Risks, and Policies in International Development Association Countries . Previously, she worked in the World Bank’s Macroeconomics, Trade, and Investment Global Practice for the Europe and Central Asia Region. Prior to that, she worked in economic consulting.
