Before starting this position on April 1, 2023, Aurélien worked as an economist in Ghana, Kazakhstan, Nepal, and Kenya. His work spanned Decentralization and Intergovernmental Fiscal Relations, Debt Management and Debt Restructuring, and Growth and Poverty Reduction Strategies. He has led and contributed to numerous flagship reports, including country strategies, economic updates, and memoranda. Aurélien joined the World Bank as a Young Professional in 2007.
