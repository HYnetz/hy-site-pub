President Malpass and Secretary Ramírez de la O discussed Mexico’s recovery from the COVID-19 pandemic, key government investment initiatives, and private capital mobilization – including through securitization and other capital market instruments – to spur inclusive and sustainable development. President Malpass and Secretary Ramírez de la O reviewed efforts to support Central American countries and address regional development challenges.
