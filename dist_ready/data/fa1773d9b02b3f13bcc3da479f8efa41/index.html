Trimor Mici Home Who We Are Experts Trimor Mici Trimor Mici Senior Operations Officer, Regulatory Efficiency, Policy Indicators Group, Development Economics Trimor Mici, a Kosovan national, has led research and managed business regulation assessment projects worldwide for more than a decade. He joined the World Bank in 2008 in the Investment Climate Department and currently holds a dual role, managing projects for the Regulatory Efficiency unit and supervising the taxation team for the Business Ready project. He holds an MBA in finance and investment from the George Washington University—where he was a Global Leaders Fellow—and a BA in business administration and finance from the American University in Bulgaria.
