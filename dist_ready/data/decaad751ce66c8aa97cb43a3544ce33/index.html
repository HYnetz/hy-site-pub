Beyond the Success: Approaches to Rural Sanitation (9 minutes) Who We Are News Video February 2, 2015 Beyond the Success: Approaches to Rural Sanitation (9 minutes) Share more close linkedin digg renren stumbleupon delicious sina Over the past ten years, the Kingdom of Cambodia has increased and improved its development efforts in almost all sectors. As a result, in 2013 poverty was reduced to a record low of 19.8%. Rural development has also progressed at a remarkable rate. However, rural sanitation remains a problem and requires continued attention. Currently, adequate rural sanitation coverage is only at 38.5%. In response to this, the World Bank's Water and Sanitation Program (WSP) has been actively supporting the Ministry of Rural Development to implement three initiatives: Sanitation Marketing Program, Sanitation Finance Program and the Behavior Change Communications Program.This video highlights the lessons learned from the implementation of these initiatives.
