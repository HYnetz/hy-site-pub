Event | 26th Disaster Risk Management Seminar organized by the Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery (GFDRR) Tokyo DRM Hub Report Apple Calendar Google Calendar Outlook Calendar Yahoo Calendar Outlook.com Calendar Office 365 Calendar Home News Events 26th Disaster Risk Management Seminar organized by the Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery (GFDRR) Tokyo DRM Hub Report Share Overview As global urbanization accelerates, with nearly 70% of the world’s population expected to live in cities by 2050, the built environment is under increasing pressure. To mitigate damage and disruption from these recurring challenges, buildings must be designed and constructed to withstand both sudden-onset and chronic hazards, while adhering to essential standards for structural safety, sustainability, and accessibility. This session will unveil the GFDRR’s new report “A Global Assessment of Building Codes – Current Status and Evolving Needs to Promote Resilient, Green, and Inclusive Buildings”, supported by the Government of Japan. This report presents findings from assessments of building codes in 22 selected countries, focusing on three priority areas: structural safety and resilience, green buildings, and universal accessibility. It offers practical insights and recommendations to guide countries in future code enhancements, strengthening implementation capacity, and improving compliance mechanism. This global study builds on a previous regional study focused on Sub-Saharan Africa . This event will be hosted by the Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery (GFDRR) and its Tokyo Disaster Risk Management (DRM) Hub as part of the Technical Knowledge Exchange on Resilient Housing Reconstruction happening in Japan from June 16-20, 2025.
