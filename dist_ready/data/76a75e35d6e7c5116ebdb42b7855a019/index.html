To ensure the reliability of the published resources on the portal, the data providers of the portal are official representatives from supranational, national and local public administrations . This space offers data providers a platform to connect, share experiences and explore topics of mutual interest. If you are a data provider of data.europa.eu, you can join this community and dive into discussions on best practices and challenges. You can also propose new topics by clicking the ‘Propose a topic’ button below to bring fresh insights to the group. Stay connected – activate notifications in your data.europa.eu personal space or receive updates via email to keep up with the latest conversations!
