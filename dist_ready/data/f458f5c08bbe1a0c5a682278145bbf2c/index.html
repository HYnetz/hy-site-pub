Universal Health Coverage and Pandemic Preparedness in Haiti Who We Are News FEATURE STORY June 23, 2025 Universal Health Coverage and Pandemic Preparedness in Haiti (2019-2023) Share more close linkedin digg renren stumbleupon delicious sina STORY HIGHLIGHTS Despite improvements in many basic health indicators, the disease burden in Haiti is largely dominated by diseases that can be effectively managed through prevention and access to basic treatment The project conducted comprehensive analyses on UHC and pandemic preparedness, covering health facility performance, maternal healthcare access, resource mapping, community health strategies, and pandemic response options The project successfully informed Haiti's health policies, helping to build a more resilient healthcare system The Universal Health Coverage (UHC) and Pandemic Preparedness initiative in Haiti, aimed to enhance the country's health system and strengthen its capacity for pandemic response. This project provided critical insights and recommendations to the Haitian government for achieving UHC and improving pandemic preparedness. Key achievements of the project include: Conducted comprehensive analyses on UHC and pandemic preparedness, covering health facility performance, maternal healthcare access, resource mapping, community health strategies, and pandemic response options. Completed the report "Enhancing Service Delivery for UHC," which provided findings and lessons learned to guide government policies on UHC and pandemic preparedness. The project has successfully informed Haiti's health policies, helping to build a more resilient healthcare system.
