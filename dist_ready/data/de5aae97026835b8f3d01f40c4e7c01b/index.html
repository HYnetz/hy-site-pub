Carolyn Pelnik Home Who We Are Experts Carolyn Pelnik Carolyn Pelnik Research Economist, Development Research Group Contact cpelnik@worldbank.org RELATED Personal Website Google Scholar Curriculum Vitae Development Research Group Finance and Private Sector Development Research Areas of Expertise Entrepreneurship - especially small and micro enterprises Market access Financial services access Poverty traps and investment indivisibilities Land markets Urban economics Carolyn is a research economist in the Finance and Private Sector Development unit of the World Bank’s Development Research Group. Her research focuses on firm growth in low- and middle-income countries, including the investment decisions of entrepreneurs, strategies for expanding market access, and the impact of expanding financial services. Some of her recent projects address firm location decisions, poverty traps and investment indivisibilities, land markets, and linking buyers to sellers in remote places.
