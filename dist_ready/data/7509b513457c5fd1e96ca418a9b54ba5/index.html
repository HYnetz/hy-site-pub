The Ice Circle - mobilizing policy and the public for ice Who We Are News Video April 7, 2013 The Ice Circle - mobilizing policy and the public for ice Share more close linkedin digg renren stumbleupon delicious sina The Ice Circle is an international forum and trust fund dedicated to generating multi-institutional and trans-boundary responses to the impact of climate change on snow and ice. The forum’s goal is to bring together policy makers, water users, community actors, and scholars concerned with the challenges represented by the impacts of climate change. The Ice Circle trust fund will enable enhanced international co-operation, integration and information sharing and promote research, mitigation and adaptation measures.
