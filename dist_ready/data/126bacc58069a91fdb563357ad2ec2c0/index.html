Chloe Fevre Home Who We Are Experts Chloe Fevre Chloe Fevre Senior Violence Prevention Specialist and the co-coordinator of the Safe and Inclusive Schools Platform Chloë Fèvre is a Senior Violence Prevention Specialist and the co-coordinator of the Safe and Inclusive Schools Platform. She leads the agenda on Safe Schools at the Bank, supporting teams in preventing violence in and around schools so that all students can safely learn. She has extensive experience working on youth violence prevention and gender-based violence prevention in the most violent contexts in Latin America and the Caribbean. Chloë is now focusing her expertise and passion on making sure no child anywhere is afraid to go to school. She can be reached at: cfevre@worldbank.org .
