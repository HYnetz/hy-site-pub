Haja Fatima: Helping a Yemeni farmer boost her production Who We Are News Video August 23, 2018 Haja Fatima: Helping a Yemeni farmer boost her production Share more close linkedin digg renren stumbleupon delicious sina When Haja Fatima lost both her husband and eldest son, she was forced to take over the family farm and try to eke out a living for herself and her 6 other children – an almost impossible challenge in conflict-ridden Yemen. Through the Small and Micro Enterprise Promotion Service (SMEPS) and the World Bank’s Emergency Crisis Response Project, Fatimah was able to receive a grant that allowed her to boost her production and her income.
