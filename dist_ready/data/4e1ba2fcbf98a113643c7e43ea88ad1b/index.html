Ten Years of the Vienna Initiative 2009-2019 Where We Work Europe and Central Asia brief BRIEF December 18, 2019 Ten Years of the Vienna Initiative 2009-2019 Email Print Tweet Share Share LinkedIn Digg 人人网 Stumble Upon Delicious 新浪微博 udhaya --> New Publication Commemorating 10 Years of the Vienna Initiative (EIB, December 2019) The Vienna Initiative came into existence in early 2009 as a venue for bringing together country authorities, official multilateral donors, and Western European banks with large exposures in Emerging Europe to discuss the economic programs being implemented by countries, with financial support from the International Monetary Fund, the European Commission, the World Bank, and the EBRD. The World Bank Group delivered over €17 billion under the two phases of the Joint IFI Action Plan during the period 2009-14. Most of the delivered financing has been in the form of signed IBRD loans. Given the specific nature of the institutions that constitute the World Bank Group, the IBRD’s contributions were mainly policy-based lending and technical assistance on deepening reforms to promote competitiveness, strengthening and diversifying financial sectors, improving energy efficiency and supporting social sector reforms. The IFC focused on recapitalizing banks, increasing access to trade finance, supporting infrastructure investment and funding for microfinance. It also provided significant value through advisory support to local banks. MIGA’s guarantees helped support the recapitalization of banks by providing political risk insurance that covered the cross-border capital injections made by foreign parent banks. This book was published by the European Investment Bank on behalf of the Vienna Initiative Steering Committee, to mark ten years of the Vienna Initiative from 2009-2019.
