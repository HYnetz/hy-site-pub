Supporting Public Procurement Efficiency in Kazakhstan Who We Are News Video October 1, 2015 Supporting Public Procurement Efficiency in Kazakhstan Share more close linkedin digg renren stumbleupon delicious sina In 2010, Kazakhstan launched its electronic Government Procurement (e-GP) system with a view to improving and facilitating transparency and effective spending of public funds. Since then, public procurement has dramatically improved - ultimately saving over $1.3 billion in budget spending. Today, the e-GP system has more than 20,000 public entities and 160,000 registered potential suppliers both from Kazakhstan and other countries. The procurement process has seen a significant reduction in time, cost, and paperwork.
