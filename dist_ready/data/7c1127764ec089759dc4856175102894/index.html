Priority Themes PROBLUE: The World Bank’s Blue Economy Program PROBLUE: Healthy Ocean, Healthy Economies, Healthy Communities Home Overview Pillars Supported Work Resources Themes News Events Partnership Council Results PILLAR 1: Fisheries and Aquaculture Improving fisheries by tackling the underlying causes of overfishing and strengthening aquaculture sustainability The overall objective of Pillar 1 is to improve fisheries governance. Activities are divided into two broad categories: Reducing overexploitation of capture fisheries, including by improving transparency, governance, and reducing the impact of harmful fisheries subsidies; Improving aquaculture, including by increasing biosecurity to prevent disease, and supporting new development models in aquaculture PILLAR 2: Marine Pollution Addressing threats posed to ocean health from marine pollution, including litter and plastics, from marine or land-based sources The overall objective of Pillar 2 is the prevention of marine pollution and litter. This requires new policies, changes from consumers and industries, investments, innovations, and private sector interventions. Pillar 2 is prioritizing activities that prevent marine plastics, including: Improving solid waste management to stop leakages; Transitioning to a more circular economy, in which products are designed for reuse and recycling, making waste more valuable; Reducing the upstream production and use of plastics PILLAR 3: Oceanic Sectors Enhancing sustainability of key oceanic sectors such as tourism, maritime transport and offshore renewable energy The overall objective of Pillar 3 is the “blueing” of oceanic sectors, to be achieved by supporting more sustainable development of both: Traditional maritime activities: e.g. shipping and ports, and tourism More innovative uses: e.g. offshore wind and desalination PILLAR 4: Seascape Management Building government capacity to manage marine resources, including nature-based solutions, and to mobilize private sector finance: The overall objective of Pillar 4 is to strengthen integrated and sustainable management of coastal and marine areas.
