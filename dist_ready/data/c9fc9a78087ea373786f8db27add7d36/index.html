Xavier Devictor Home Who We Are Experts Xavier Devictor Xavier Devictor 2023 World Development Report Co-Director AREAS OF EXPERTISE Aid Effectiveness Conflict Conflict and fragile states Migration Policies and Jobs Xavier Devictor is the Co-Director for the World Development Report 2023. He was previously Manager in the Fragility, Conflict and Violence Group at the World Bank, where he was leading strategic, analytical, and operational work on forced displacement. Xavier joined the World Bank as a Young Professional and has broad operational experience across regions, including as Country Manager for Poland and the Baltic Countries. He has worked in South Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and the Balkans. Earlier in his career, Xavier worked for UNHCR in the former Yugoslavia, where he managed humanitarian programs and led humanitarian convoys into conflict areas.
