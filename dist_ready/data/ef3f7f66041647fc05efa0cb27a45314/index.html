Jobs The MENAAP region faces an immense jobs challenge. Your working-age population is growing far faster than jobs are being created. The region’s working-age population—now about 380 million—will increase by 40% over the next 25 years, but economic growth has been low and not job intensive. Our goal is more private sector jobs, faster. Learn More Water The World Bank is dedicated to supporting MENAAP countries in taking strong steps to ensure a water-secure future for their citizens.
