President Malpass, Councillor Parmelin, and Chairman Jordan discussed support for Ukraine and neighboring countries and the global impact of the war. President Malpass, Councillor Parmelin, and Chairman Jordan exchanged views on inflation and the surge in prices for energy, food and fertilizer and the importance of increasing energy supplies, promoting trade, and building stronger food systems. President Malpass was pleased to mark the 30 th anniversary of Switzerland joining the World Bank and thanked Councillor Parmelin for Switzerland’s generous and increased contribution to IDA20. President Malpass updated Councillor Parmelin and Chairman Jordan on the WBG’s work on global public goods, fragility, climate change, COVID-19, and crisis preparedness. They also discussed the challenges facing debt sustainability and transparency and the WBG and IMF’s cooperation in encouraging steps to implement the G20’s Common Framework.
