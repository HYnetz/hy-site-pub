The outreach workshop is intended to enable the business community to participate more effectively in business opportunities created through government- and donor-funded projects in Ukraine. Further, the workshop aims to provide useful information to the business community on the opportunities in Ukraine emergency assistance and reconstruction program. Specifically, the following topics will be covered: Brief on IFIs’ (World Bank, EBRD, EIB, and CEB) portfolio in Ukraine Programs supported by the Ukraine Relief, Recovery, Reconstruction and Reform Trust Fund (URTF) Key aspects of procurement rules and procedures of Ukraine and the IFIs Tips for preparing acceptable bids and proposals Mechanism for resolving bidders’ complaints Briefing on the government e-procurement system ”Prozorro” – key upgrading done to facilitate participation of bidders Challenges faced by businesses in execution of contracts in Ukraine OVERVIEW RELATED DATE: April 16, 2025 TIME: 1:00PM – 5:00PM Kyiv time LANGUAGE: English with simultaneous translation to Ukrainian Join Online (Webex) Ukraine Relief, Recovery, Reconstruction and Reform Trust Fund (URTF) The World Bank in Ukraine Event Details Date: April 16, 2025 Time: 09:43 AM - 09:43 AM ET Location: Hybrid Status message Thank you for requesting an event reminder for email-address.
