The workshop aims to: - Acquaint construction firms with the project’s scope of Work and upcoming bidding opportunities, including providing an overview of the scope of work, procurement procedures, procurement procedures, and timeline. - Gain insight into market capabilities, innovations, and best practices. - Present strategic procurement directions and options and solicit feedback and concerns, if any. - Identify potential risks and opportunities for efficiency and sustainability. - Encourage wide participation and competition in the procurement process. - Identify the right evaluation criteria and weightings. - Obtain specific feedback from the Market on the Procurement Strategy for the above packages and identify potential firms' interest in participating in the bidding.
