Alessio Zanelli Home Who We Are Experts Alessio Zanelli Alessio Zanelli Senior Private Sector Specialist, Regulatory Efficiency, Policy Indicators Group Alessio Zanelli, an Italian national, joined the World Bank Group in 2009. Prior to his current role, he served as a Senior Private Sector Specialist in the Global Investment Climate team, advising national and subnational governments on reforms to strengthen the business environment and promote private sector growth. He has extensive experience as both a team and technical lead across Europe and the Middle East, as well as Sub-Saharan Africa and East Asia. Over his tenure at the World Bank Group, Zanelli has contributed to nonlending and lending operations in more than 30 countries. He has also coauthored policy notes, reform memoranda, and blogs, and led subnational benchmarking projects in Egypt and Italy. Zanelli holds a degree in political science, with a focus on comparative and international law, from the University of Trieste, and an MSc in European political economy from the London School of Economics.
