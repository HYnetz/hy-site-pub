This year’s focus spotlights what’s ahead: leveraging innovation to boost performance and transparency, moving beyond cost to deliver enterprise value, aligning purchasing power with the SDGs, and strengthening risk management for resilient, future-ready supply chains. We look forward to open dialogue, sharing best practices, and forging partnerships that harmonize standards and amplify our collective impact. Over the two days, we will explore how to harness digital innovation, strengthen category management, drive sustainable outcomes, and enhance resilience across our organizations.
