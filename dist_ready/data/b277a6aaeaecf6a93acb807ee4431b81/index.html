As China’s challenges have changed, the Bank Group strategy has evolved both substantively and geographically, with greater focus on knowledge, innovation, and the environment, and more emphasis on lagging central and western regions. The new China Country Partnership Strategy provides a framework for Bank Group engagement with China for the next four years. It is aligned with China’s 12 th Five-Year Plan, and informed by the study, China 2030, prepared jointly by the World Bank and the Development Research Center of the State Council.
