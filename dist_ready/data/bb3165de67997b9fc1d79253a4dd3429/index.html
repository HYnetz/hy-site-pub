This research, which is of interest to the wider academic community, is shared via relevant World Bank and external blog platforms. Competition and firm recovery post-COVID-19 in Europe and Central Asia DECEMBER 7, 2021 | This study examines the process of reallocation in 23 countries in Europe and Central Asia (ECA) to understand the relationship between firms' pre-COVID-19 labor productivity and their performance during the pandemic. Opening-up for a strong economic recovery: Lessons from the first wave of COVID-19 DECEMBER 22, 2020 | In our recent paper we provide evidence on the effects of different reopening trajectories, their timing, and speed of reopening on economic activity, using high-frequency data on electricity consumption. A banking sector pandemic AUGUST 21, 2020 | The spread of COVID-19 represents an unpresented global shock, with the disease itself and mitigation efforts—such as social distancing measures and partial and national lockdown measures—having a significant impact on the economy. The sooner, the better: Early lockdowns save more lives and livelihoods JULY 9, 2020 | This column analyses high-frequency proxies of economic activity and suggests that lockdowns led to a decline of about 10% in economic activity across Europe and Central Asia. Improving social protection by reaping the gains from international migration September 27, 2019 | This column argues that a more feasible policy is to let citizens in host countries rent out their right-to-work for a period, financed by foreigners purchasing time-bound work permits.
