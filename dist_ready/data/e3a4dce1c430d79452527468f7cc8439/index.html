Duong Le Home Who We Are Experts Duong Le Duong Le Economist, East Asia and Pacific, EAP RELATED Curriculum Vitae Duong Trung Le is an Economist in the Office of the Chief Economist for East Asia and the Pacific at the World Bank. His research focuses on the labor market and enterprise performance in developing countries, and on environmental aspects of sustainable socio-economic development. He has published in peer-reviewed economics journals such as Journal of Development Economics, Journal of Economic Behavior and Organization, and World Development. Before joining the World Bank, Duong was a Postdoctoral Associate at Duke University.
