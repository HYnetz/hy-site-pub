Share Overview Ready. Set. LEADS! LEADS Continuing Education Series Join us for the first webinar in the Ready. Set. LEADS! Continuing Education series . These webinars will highlight how key research insights are being used to support project designs, and how the scaling of effective ideas happens in practice. We’ll kick off with a deep dive into how smart subsidies can boost adoption of modern technologies . New evidence from Mozambique shows that e-vouchers dramatically improve uptake and cost-effectiveness of input subsidies. The webinar will describe the research insights [ Learn ], how this evidence is being applied in operations [ Adapt ], and how these approaches can inform fertilizer subsidy reforms across countries and sectors [ Scale ].
