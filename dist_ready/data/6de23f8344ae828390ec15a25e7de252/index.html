Event | Ukraine: Early Market Engagement for Procurement of Civil Works in Health Sector Apple Calendar Google Calendar Outlook Calendar Yahoo Calendar Outlook.com Calendar Office 365 Calendar Home News Events Ukraine: Early Market Engagement for Procurement of Civil Works in Health Sector Share Overview The Ministry of Health of Ukraine in partnership with the World Bank are pleased to invite you to Early Market Engagement Workshop dedicated to the reconstruction, capital repair, and construction of healthcare facilities under the joint “Health Enhancement And Lifesaving” (HEAL Ukraine) project. The workshop will provide an opportunity for companies to learn about and engage in the recovery and rebuilding of Ukraine’s healthcare infrastructure. The session will highlight current and upcoming procurement opportunities, including the Reconstruction of Mykolaiv Regional Children's Clinical Hospital, New construction of a medical-rehabilitation building with a children's mental health center for the Volyn Regional Territorial Medical Association for the Protection of Motherhood and Childhood, Reconstruction of a non-residential building into a rehabilitation center in Uzhhorod. These works are financed by the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (IBRD) and the Council of Europe Development Bank (CEB) as part of the HEAL Ukraine project. We look forward to engaging with your company to learn more about your expertise in construction and rehabilitation and invite you to participate in the Q&A session designed for interested stakeholders.
