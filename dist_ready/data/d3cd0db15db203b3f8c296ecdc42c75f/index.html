World Bank Gender Strategy 2024-2030 Gender At-A-Glance The World Bank Group (WBG) works with public- and private-sector clients to accelerate gender equality to end poverty on a livable planet. Growing evidence shows how removing gender barriers unlocks economic productivity, reduces poverty, deepens social cohesion and enhances well-being and prosperity for current and future generations. The WBG supports innovation, financing and collective action to end gender-based violence, elevate human capital, expand and enable economic opportunities and engage women as leaders. The WBG employs a multifaceted strategy to achieve outcomes and impact . One in three women worldwide experience physical or sexual violence by an intimate partner or sexual violence by a non-partner over the course of their life. Young women are the most at-risk group. This pattern holds irrespective of country income classification. Women’s economic prospects remain constrained. Across all regions of the world, women’s labor force participation remains below that of men. Over the last 25 years, women’s participation in national parliaments has increased but is far from reaching equality.
