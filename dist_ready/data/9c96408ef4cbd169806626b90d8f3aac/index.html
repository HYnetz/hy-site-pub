Reducing Vulnerability and Increasing Opportunities Who We Are News Video March 1, 2013 Reducing Vulnerability and Increasing Opportunities Share more close linkedin digg renren stumbleupon delicious sina Education and health for women and girls in Laos, in recent decades, are improving. However, women are still dying of childbirth and girls continue to drop out of school at alarming rates, especially in rural areas. Women don’t hold decision-making power in their households. Economic opportunities for women, although improving, remain constrained. It’s clear that gender inequality hinders development. Watch this video clip to see how Laos is trying to overcome these challenges, by implementing gender policies and investing more in the well-being of Lao women and girls.
