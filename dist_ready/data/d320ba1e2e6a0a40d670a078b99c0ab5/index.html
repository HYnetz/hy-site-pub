Event | Sharing Prosperity: Policy Innovations to Tackle Economic Inequality Apple Calendar Google Calendar Outlook Calendar Yahoo Calendar Outlook.com Calendar Office 365 Calendar Home News Events Sharing Prosperity: Policy Innovations to Tackle Economic Inequality Share Overview Agenda Globally, one in five people live in highly unequal societies. Forty-nine countries, encompassing about 22 percent of the global population, had a Gini index above 40 according to the latest round of household data. Concerns about the impact of rising inequality have intensified in these and other countries. As part of our new vision to create a world free of poverty on a livable planet, the World Bank is now explicitly monitoring inequality. This event reflected on the impact economic inequality has on development and development policy, leading into the 11th Meeting of the Society for the Study of Economic Inequality (ECINEQ) , co-hosted by the World Bank and George Washington University. The ECINEQ meeting brings together leading scholars from around the world to discuss recent research and findings on economic inequality.
