Primary education enrollment reached 90% in 2019, up from 81% in 2009. Yet the path to education is not free of obstacles. Financial burdens weigh heavily on families, often stopping children from continuing their secondary education. Targeted solutions are needed to lift these economic pressures and pave the way for learning. Recommendations include stepping up investment in early childhood and primary education, enhancing teacher effectiveness, and making the distribution of resources more equitable.
