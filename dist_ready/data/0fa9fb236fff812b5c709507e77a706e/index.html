John Arzinos Home Who We Are Experts John Arzinos John Arzinos Consultant, Sexual Orientation and Gender Identity (SOGI), World Bank Group AREAS OF EXPERTISE Social Development Social Protection and Labor Social Safeguards John (Ioannis) Arzinos joined the Development Economics (DEC) Vice Presidency of the World Bank in 2017 and has since worked on many projects, including Women, Business and the Law (WBL), Business Enterprise Surveys, and Global Indicators of Regulatory Governance (GIRG). He has been part of the SOGI Core Team since 2018, where he has been working on the global Environmental and Social Framework (ESF) engagement and SOGI-specific research. He is also working on Social Protection in Africa and on Regulatory Advisory Services in Europe. Prior to joining the World Bank, he worked with organizations such as the US Chamber of Commerce and Uber Technologies in the United States. He holds a master’s degree in international relations and international economics from the Johns Hopkins School of Advanced International Studies (SAIS) and a bachelor’s degree in international relations from the University of Piraeus in Greece.
