President Malpass thanked President Cortizo for hosting the Bank’s new hub in Panama City, which will allow for strengthened support to World Bank Group clients and partners in the region. President Malpass and President Cortizo discussed the increased migration flows in the region and the need for assisting countries that are hosting migrants. President Malpass welcomed Panama’s progress on its COVID-19 vaccine deployment and its work on the AML/CFT agenda. The World Bank stands ready to support further progress in both areas.
