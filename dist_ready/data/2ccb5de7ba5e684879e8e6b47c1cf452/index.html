Event | Africa-Japan Higher Education Partnership Matchmaking Seminar Apple Calendar Google Calendar Outlook Calendar Yahoo Calendar Outlook.com Calendar Office 365 Calendar Home News Events Africa-Japan Higher Education Partnership Matchmaking Seminar Share Overview Since 2018, the World Bank has been facilitating Africa-Japan higher education partnerships through World Bank-financed initiatives such as the Africa Centers of Excellence (ACE) and the Partnership for Skills in Applied Sciences, Engineering, and Technology (PASET) Regional Scholarship and Innovation Fund (RSIF). These flagship programs aim to enhance the quality of scientific research and innovation in priority areas across Sub-Saharan Africa, while fostering regional and international collaboration to strengthen education and research. In response to growing interest from Japanese universities and industries in partnering with African institutions, two matching events were held in 2018 and 2019, resulting in the signing of over ten Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs). This year, with TICAD once again taking place in Japan, the World Bank has invited interested ACEs to explore new collaboration opportunities with Japanese academic and industrial partners. In this context, the World Bank organized a special seminar focused on partnership development with participating ACEs. Nine centers from universities in Ethiopia, Ghana, Malawi (three universities), Nigeria, and Togo joined the seminar to explore potential partnerships. Each ACE delivered a brief presentation, followed by matchmaking sessions conducted both in person and virtually. Objectives The objectives of the seminar are to: (i) provide an overview of the Africa Centers of Excellence (ACEs) and explore potential partnership opportunities, and (ii) engage in discussions on research collaboration, innovation, and student/faculty exchanges. The seminar aims to facilitate meaningful dialogue that may lead to the establishment of concrete collaboration opportunities.
