This group encompasses a diverse range of users, including non-governmental organisations, international organisations, private sector companies, academic institutions, students and more. As key drivers of the data economy, data reusers play a vital role in unlocking the potential of open data and creating meaningful impact. This space is designed to foster the exchange of best practices and improve collaboration between data reusers and suppliers. Here, you can explore and share innovative ways of using and reusing data, discuss challenges and opportunities and learn how your peers are leveraging open data. You can join conversations and follow them or propose additional topics of your interest. Join In order to be part of the collaboration channel.
