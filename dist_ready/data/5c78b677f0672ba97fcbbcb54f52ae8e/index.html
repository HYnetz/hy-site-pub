We also updated total U.S. production capacity to 27,575,000 pounds of U 3 O 8 per year and changed the operating status for Burke Hollow and the Goliad ISR Uranium Project to permitted and licensed . --> Second-quarter 2025 U.S. production of uranium concentrate (U 3 O 8 ) in the second quarter of 2025 totaled 437,238 pounds U 3 O 8 , an 41% increase from first quarter 2025 production of 310,533 pounds U 3 O 8 . This quarter’s total uranium production occurred at 5 facilities, 3 in Wyoming (Nichols Ranch ISR Project, Smith Ranch-Highland Operation, and Lost Creek Project), 1 in Texas (Alta Mesa Project) and 1 in Utah (White Mesa Mill).
