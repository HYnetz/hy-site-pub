Although the country has initiated critical reforms aimed at structural transformation to boost the private sector, promote innovation, modernize the economy, and address vulnerabilities, the economic impact of the COVID-19 pandemic threatens this transformation and emphasizes the need to ensure the momentum of reform efforts endure. The Mission A C-JET funded grant by the World Bank in Ethiopia examined firm-specific constraints and gaps that affect technology adoption for general business practices and sector-specific production processes across diverse levels of complexity; from well-established and widely available technologies to cutting edge innovations. A report was developed on challenges and opportunities for technology adoption in Ethiopia. Through these activities and more, this grant supported policies that boost technology adoption and advancement and raise labor productivity and competitiveness of firms.
