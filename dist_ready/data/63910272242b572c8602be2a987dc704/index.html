This is part of a growing international movement focusing on the skills required for students to succeed in a rapidly changing, digital society. The World Bank works on this issue in many countries, including the Russian Federation. The World Bank supports the recently-formed partnership between the Moscow City University, Rybakov Foundation, Projectoria NGO, and the ROBBO company. The main goal of this partnership is to engage more Russian children in coding (including collaborative) and computational thinking through organizing a series of Collab Challenges , which is a team coding competition for schoolchildren, including hackathons and webinars on coding for youth.
