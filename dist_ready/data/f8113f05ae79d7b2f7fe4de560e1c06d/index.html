QEDS: Quarterly External Debt Statistics Debt Statistics Quarterly External Debt Statistics (QEDS) Quarterly External Debt Statistics (QEDS) Home About Statistics Publications Methodology Debt Events Contact Us FAQ The QEDS databases brings together detailed external debt data of countries that subscribe to the IMF’s Special Data Dissemination Standard (SDDS) and a selected number of countries that participate in the IMF’s General Data Dissemination System (GDDS). QEDS provides the quarterly external debt position broken down by sector, maturity, instruments and currency. Explore the QEDS data tables below or for more data and information you may visit: Databank: SDDS (Timeseries) Databank: GDDS (Timeseries) Methodology Publication calendar QEDS GDDS Data Tables SDDS Participants GDDS Participants « » Country Tables Cross Country Tables Country Metadata Cross Country Metadata The QEDS GDDS database, jointly developed by the World Bank and the International Monetary Fund, are in-line with the classifications and definitions of the 2013 External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users ( 2013 EDS Guide ) and Sixth Edition of Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual ( BPM6 ). The benefit of bringing together comparable external debt data is to facilitate macroeconomic analysis and cross-country data comparison.
