Devaki Ghose Home Who We Are Experts Devaki Ghose Devaki Ghose Research Economist, Development Research Group Contact dghose@worldbank.org RELATED Researcher Website Curriculum Vitae Development Research Group Trade and International Integration Research Google Scholar Page Areas of Expertise International Economics Trade Policy Infrastructure Migration Devaki Ghose is an Economist in the Trade and International Integration Unit of the Development Research Group. Her primary fields of research are international and urban economics. Her research explores how globalization, trade, and migration policies shape the spatial and distributional dimensions of economic growth. She is particularly interested in studying how structural constraints — such as high migration costs, trade and regulatory frictions, and gender-specific barriers — mediate firm and worker responses to global and local shocks.
