President Malpass thanked Jordan for playing an important role in hosting and providing health and educational services to more than 3 million refugees from the region. He also commended Jordan for its successful cash transfer program, and noted the importance of pro-growth reforms that stimulate private sector activity. President Malpass and Ministers Shraideh and Al-Ississ discussed shared development priorities, including creating a business climate that enables private sector growth, job creation, energy sector sustainability, and regional integration.
