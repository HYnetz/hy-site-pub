President Malpass congratulated Secretary Truss on her new roles as Foreign Secretary and the UK’s Governor of the World Bank Group. President Malpass highlighted the World Bank Group’s work in response to the COVID-19 pandemic and to accelerate vaccine access for developing countries. President Malpass and Secretary Truss discussed shared development priorities, including trade, gender, and climate in the lead-up to COP26. President Malpass also highlighted the importance of country platforms. President Malpass welcomed the UK’s strong support for IDA and explained IDA’s critical role in strengthening resilience and crisis preparedness in the world’s poorest countries.
