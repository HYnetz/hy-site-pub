Advancing systems and supporting GovTech enablers to prevent corruption and facilitate meaningful roll-out of the envisioned E-Justice System Armenia This project supports core systems and GovTech enablers in anticorruption and justice in Armenia. Scaling Up the Digital Community (CDD) Procurement Brazil Community-driven development is a key operational strategy for many governments and international aid agencies. The Economic Costs of Non-Competitive Public Procurement and State Capture Bulgaria This project seeks to design and set up a mechanism for the early detection of public procurement malpractice in Bulgaria, in order to address concerns about state capture and public spending efficiency. Outputs include a detection mechanism using public procurement contracts from the opentender.eu database, methodological note to enable replication of the methodology, an analytical report, and training seminar for government stakeholders. Modernization and Integration of the Human Resource Management Information System Cambodia This project assists Cambodia's Ministry of Civil Service in modernizing its existing Human Resource Management Information System (HRMIS), including roadmap development and online payroll module enhancement. GovTech Innovation Challenge Overview The World Bank's Institutions Global Department, in collaboration with the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs in Switzerland (SECO) and Trust Valley , presents the GovTech Innovation Challenge. This initiative accelerates technology adoption in government services by leveraging the Swiss digital trust ecosystem. Supported by the World Bank Trust Fund's Governance & Institutions Umbrella Program , the pilot program offers a dynamic platform for testing innovative technological solutions in the public sector. The inaugural Challenge focuses on the Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA), connecting them with cutting-edge companies to tackle specific tax administration challenges. Through a competitive application process, global technology firms will propose digital solutions to these pre-defined issues, aiming to enhance government efficiency, transparency, and responsiveness. Program benefits for companies Gain exposure to public sector clients that receive support from the World Bank Receive executive level training in Switzerland on key public-private collaboration aspects Engage with the public sector to design a proof of concept. Selected companies will attend an intensive boot camp in Switzerland's Trust Valley, a hub for digital trust innovation. There, they will refine their solutions and gain valuable skills in public-private collaboration through workshops, expert mentorship, and networking events. The initiative aims to transform public services and set a precedent for future GovTech programs. The Challenge will extend its impact by sharing developed solutions with other World Bank member countries, enabling multiple countries to benefit from these advancements.
