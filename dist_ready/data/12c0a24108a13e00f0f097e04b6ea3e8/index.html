Entrepreneurship Entrepreneurship Database The Entrepreneurship Database collects administrative data from business registries and statistics agencies in 180 economies. Gender disaggregated data was also collected in 95 economies to assess the gap between female and male entrepreneurs. The data collection was completed by the end of 2023.
