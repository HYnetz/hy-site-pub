These resources support countries to improve CPD through one-to-one coaching, group training and workshops, and other approaches delivered in person, remotely, or in hybrid formats. The program helped countries design, implement, and evaluate high-quality, evidence-aligned CPD programs and systems. The Coach program builds on Teach , the World Bank’s suite of open-access classroom observation tools. While Teach helps countries assess teaching practices to inform teaching quality improvements. The Coach program translates those insights into action—providing tools and guidance to plan and deliver effective CPD to improve teaching. The SUNSET grants supported countries to strengthen education systems and improve student learning by collecting data on teaching practices using the Teach suite of classroom observation tools, and enhancing the quality of teacher CPD, leveraging resources from the World Bank’s Coach program. The grants also contributed to the global evidence base on CPD: from 2022 to 2024, twenty-four countries received SUNSET grants. SUNSET Grants at a Glance 24 countries (Pakistan, India, Romania, Djibouti, Somalia, Tanzania, Uzbekistan, Cameroon, Mexico, Burundi, El Salvador, Honduras, Cote d’Ivoire, Kiribati, Tuvalu, Indonesia, Cabo Verde, Armenia, Algeria, Morocco, Brazil, Central African Republic, Eswatini, and Nepal) were awarded Teach-Coach SUNSET grants totaling almost $5.5 million.
