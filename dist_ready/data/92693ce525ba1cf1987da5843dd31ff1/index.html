Event | Early Market Engagement for the Improvement of Connectivity in Interzonal Hospitals of the Province of Buenos Aires Apple Calendar Google Calendar Outlook Calendar Yahoo Calendar Outlook.com Calendar Office 365 Calendar Home News Events Early Market Engagement for the Improvement of Connectivity in Interzonal Hospitals of the Province of Buenos Aires Share Overview The purpose of this process is the turnkey procurement of goods and services to establish and operationalize a Local Area Network (LAN) in eight strategic interzonal hospitals: HIGA Penna (Bahía Blanca), HIGA Eva Perón (San Martín), HIGA Presidente Perón (Avellaneda), HIGA Gandulfo (Lomas de Zamora), HIGA San Martín (La Plata), HIGA Sor María Ludovica (La Plata), HIGA San Roque (Gonnet), and HIGA San Juan de Dios (La Plata). This initiative includes not only the delivery of new, fully functional equipment, but also the execution of installation works, adaptation of technology rooms, provision of UPS and racks, configuration of core and access switches, network points, and wireless network controllers. All activities will comply with standards for interoperability, energy efficiency, and cybersecurity. Additionally, the contract will include training for provincial and hospital teams.The objectives of this early market engagement are: - To assess the interest of market participants in the turnkey supply and installation contract for the LAN network in eight provincial hospitals. - To gather information on technical requirements, potential challenges, and opportunities related to the implementation of the contract. - To identify suppliers, integrators, developers, and technical partners from the private sector who are likely to participate in the contract. - To optimize the technical, operational, and contractual structure of the contract prior to the request for bids. The hybrid event will be conducted in Spanish language.
