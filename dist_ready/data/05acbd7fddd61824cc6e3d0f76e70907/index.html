Elena leads and contributes to air quality assessments and policy reform initiatives, particularly in Central Asia and Europe. She has played a key role in flagship reports such as CCDRs and the Global Report on Access to Clean Air. Elena collaborates across sectors and regions on green growth strategies, labor market modeling, and integrated development planning with emphasis on resilience, productivity enhancement, and the co-benefits of improved air quality.
