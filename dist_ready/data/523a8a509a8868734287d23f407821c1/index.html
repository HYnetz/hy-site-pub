However, the predominance of monoculture forests has left them highly susceptible to diseases and pests, while rapid economic development has made ecological conditions fragile. As a result, these rich natural resources face biodiversity degradation, soil erosion, and flash floods. To help China address these challenges, the World Bank approved a $150 million loan in 2019 for the Forest Ecosystem Improvement in the Upper Reaches of Yangtze River Basin Program . Implemented in Sichuan Province, this program was the first Program-for-Results (PforR) operation in China’s forestry sector supported by the World Bank. PforR loans link disbursement of funds to the achievement of specific results and support institutional building and innovation.
