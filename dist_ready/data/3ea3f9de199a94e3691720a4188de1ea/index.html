Despite progress in adopting cleaner fuels like LPG and biogas, many impoverished households still rely on these polluting methods. This video sheds light on the innovative efforts of women-led Self Help Groups (SHGs) to introduce improved stoves, reducing health risks for women and children and cutting down on Bihar’s ambient air pollution.
