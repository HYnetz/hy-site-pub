Messaging standards in Fast Payments Who We Are News Video May 14, 2024 Messaging Standards in Fast Payments Share more close linkedin digg renren stumbleupon delicious sina Also known as instant or real-time payments, fast payments are characterized by the instant transmission of the payment message and by the immediate availability of funds to the beneficiary on a 24/7/365 basis. The mass adoption of fast payments and changing business dynamics around the globe have created an environment in which upgrades, along with new implementations, are becoming a key trend in the payments industry. In this context, messaging standards -which define the syntax, structure, and semantics of messages exchanged between two or more parties to communicate and transfer information- play a critical role. Globally, three main types of messaging standards are used in FPS: ISO 8583, ISO 20022, and proprietary.
