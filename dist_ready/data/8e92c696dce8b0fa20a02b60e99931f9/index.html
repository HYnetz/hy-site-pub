Crude oil and natural gas proved reserves were effectively flat for the group from 2023. Several companies substantially increased spending on proved and unproved reserves acquisition. Cash from operations decreased in real terms by 10% and spending on investing and financing activities decreased 19% from 2023. Distributions to shareholders via dividends and share repurchases as a share of cash from operations continue to be elevated.
