Collette Mari Wheeler Home Who We Are Experts Collette Mari Wheeler Collette Mari Wheeler Senior Economist, Development Economics, DEC Contact cwheeler@worldbank.org RELATED Prospects Group website AREAS OF EXPERTISE Global Economy Growth Fiscal Policy Collette Mari Wheeler is a Senior Economist in the World Bank’s Prospects Group. She is one of the lead authors of the corporate flagship Global Economic Prospects report, which presents the World Bank’s view on the global economy. In 2024, she coauthored The Great Reversal: Prospects, Risks, and Policies in International Development Association Countries . Previously, she worked in the World Bank’s Macroeconomics, Trade, and Investment Global Practice for the Europe and Central Asia Region. Prior to that, she worked in economic consulting.
