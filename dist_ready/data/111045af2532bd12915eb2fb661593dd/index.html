The meeting was also attended by His Excellency Ambassador of Mali Sekou Berte and other officials representing ministries and departments of the country. The discussion covered a range of issues, including the re-engagement of the WBG in Mali, problems and new opportunities for the WBG projects in FCV countries, the political and economic situation in Mali, as well as bilateral relations between Russia and Mali in trade and energy sectors.
