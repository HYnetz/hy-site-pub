The National Recovery Program Bureau (NRPB) intends to conduct an Early Market Engagement to establish the level of market interest in participating in the procurement process for contract number SX-NRPB-388584-CW-RFB Construction of sewage network & WWTP in Sint Maarten to extend the coverage of piped gravity sewer to areas not covered by the existing sewerage network and to upgrade/optimize the existing Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) with operational readiness to accommodate the increased sewage flow from residences/institutions/businesses that will be received at the plant in the near future through the extended sewer network. It is expected that the Request for Bids will be made in April 2026 . NRPB is running the present Early Market Engagement exercise on behalf of the Government of Sint Maarten. NRPB is inviting suitably qualified and experienced bidders who may have an interest in this project to complete a questionnaire to assist us to gain a better understanding of the feasibility of their requirements, to find the most appropriate approach, to seek the capacity of the market to deliver and to identify any possible risks or issues that may not have previously been considered. Interested contractors may obtain further information from the National Recovery Program Bureau at the address below during office hours 0900 to 1600 hours . Interested contractors may register to view and download the ‘Information For Bidders’ document in English on the NRPB Bonfire e-Procurement website at: https://nrpbsxm.bonfirehub.com/portal/?tab=openOpportunities . Clarifications regarding this Early Market Engagement (EME) may be sought through the available communication tools in the Messages section of the opportunity - https://nrpbsxm.bonfirehub.com/portal/?tab=openOpportunities within the related opportunity listing. Firms must be registered and logged in to ask clarification questions. Questionnaires duly filled in may be submitted through the online Bonfire Portal at: https://nrpbsxm.bonfirehub.com . Submission materials should be prepared in the file format listed under Requested Information for this opportunity in the Bonfire Portal. The deadline for the submissions is 20 th October 2025 at 5 pm AST. For more information, please see here .
