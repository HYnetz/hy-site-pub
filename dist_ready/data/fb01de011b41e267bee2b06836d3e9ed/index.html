Mark your calendars for Friday, 12 September 2025, from 10:00 to 11:00 CEST. In this webinar, you will learn about the deployment of the Common European Tourism Data Space (ETDS) and how it is supporting the future of tourism through secure, interoperable, and decentralised data sharing. An expert from the DEPLOYTOUR project will share valuable insights into the development of the data space, including its objectives, best practices, and lessons learned. The session will also present two practical case studies - Pleiades and EONA-X - demonstrating how the ETDS is being applied in real-world tourism contexts across Europe. These examples will highlight how data spaces can unlock public value, encourage cross-border collaboration, and advance the tourism sector’s digital and green transitions. The webinar will conclude with a Q&A session, where our speakers will address your questions on this topic. From the data.europa academy, we are creating this conversation to hear from your about your experience with this webinar and the content that was presented. We look forward to the conversation!
